Usage Guidelines

Best practices for implementing breadcrumbs in your interface.

  • Place breadcrumbs at the top of the page, above the main content
  • Use breadcrumbs for pages that are at least three levels deep in the site hierarchy
  • Keep breadcrumb labels short and descriptive
  • The current page should be the last item and should not be a link
  • Consider using the home icon instead of text for the first item
  • Ensure breadcrumbs are responsive and wrap properly on smaller screens
